About Sweet Crumbs

Our story, our passion, our bakers.

A Little Bake Shop with Big Flavor

Sweet Crumbs Bake Shop started as a small family kitchen in North La Union. What began with weekend bread baking has grown into a community favorite known for soft pandesal, moist cakes, and warm cookies.

Every morning our bakers start early to prepare fresh batches using quality ingredients and traditional recipes passed down through generations.

What Makes Us Special

  • Freshly baked every day
  • No preservatives or artificial flavors
  • Local ingredients whenever possible
  • Custom cakes for birthdays and events
  • Friendly service and fair prices
